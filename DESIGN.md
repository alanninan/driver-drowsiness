# Dashboard design

Purpose: operate a local webcam research demonstration with legible state and measurement history.
The supplied brief determines the flow. Routine styling is resolved within the user's approval to build.

Warm paper background, deep pine header, dark camera well, quiet hairline separators.
Use a humanist sans for labels; tabular numerals for measurements. Dense signal rows beside
a large camera surface, with a full-width timeline below. No decorative metric-card grid.
The signature is a labelled three-stage warning rail sharing its colors with the fatigue trace.
Ready, calibrating, warming, unknown, alert, fatigued and critical are distinct text states.
Unobserved periods are gaps in the chart. Synthetic demo data is prominently labelled.
Keyboard focus is visible. Controls stack on phones and charts preserve readable labels.
